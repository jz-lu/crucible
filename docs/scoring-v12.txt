CRUCIBLE — PROVISIONAL POINTS POLICY v12
Decision date: 26 November 2026
Policy ID: crucible-points-v12

Every valid correct receipt that strictly advances the global recovery frontier
of its exactly enrolled scheme release earns its account 100 points. The same
account may earn another 100 for a later frontier advance. A multi-stage jump is
one advance, not one award per crossed stage. Ties, repeats, copied recoveries
and filling lower holes earn nothing. Ordinary personal progress earns zero.

Receipt eligibility, original configuration enrollment, server-time/receipt-ID
ordering and archived-release separation are unchanged from v11. Reviewer
receipts still establish the factual frontier, but reviewers earn no points and
remain excluded from rankings. First-to-reach and personal-record displays keep
their compact highest-record view. Legacy milestone flags identify every earning
advance. Family score totals aggregate all advances; legacy first-award fields
still identify the first earning receipt, not an exclusive source of that total.

This is deliberately provisional. Progressions with more configurations can
provide more opportunities, and walking intermediate stages can earn more than
one direct jump. It rewards recorded frontier advances, not proven algorithmic
novelty, equal compute, or comparable cryptographic strength. Do not add artificial
stages to generate awards. Future calibration may use frozen progress values.
New configurations/releases still need explicit scoring enrollment; changes to
navigation alone neither rescale awards nor turn old receipts into new work.

Totals are derived from existing immutable receipts under the current policy.
No account, receipt, note, prior result or attribution is erased or rewritten.
This local update requires no schema migration. A future policy can recompute
scores from the same evidence. Current admitted-scheme and bounty award amounts remain unchanged. Note
support is updated by the refinement below. v11 is preserved as historical policy.

Note support UI refinement, 26 November 2026
One current upvote per account per note earns its author one point. Posting or
publishing alone earns none. Support policy crucible-note-support-v2 counts
legacy values 1 and 2 equally; value 2 remains accepted for older clients and
exact retries, but is no longer offered as a distinct level. Existing records
are preserved. List priority, displayed supporter counts and author points use
one point per supporting account. Human clicks toggle 0/1 without a dialog.
