CRUCIBLE — CLICK FEEDBACK RULES (2026-11-26)

Navigation and text actions change color on hover/focus. They do not gain an
underline, glow or lift. This includes header tabs, wordmark, View all, notebook
and chart navigation, breadcrumbs and How points work. Inline links in reading
text and source references may retain underlines for legibility.

Buttons and icon controls change fill/border. Clickable rows and cards change
fill or strengthen their outline. Selected tabs, active filters and keyboard
focus have separate persistent indicators. Open, closed and Highlighted bounty
tags share the same pill geometry, type size and padding.

Directional action arrows move 4px in their indicated direction on hover/focus.
The presentation preserves native controls, labels and handlers. Get started
also has an occasional idle arrow nudge and a soft beacon. Its entrance follows
the tagline reveal with a fade only; bounty glow and challenge shimmer then
introduce the two
panels. The beacon uses blurred layers with soft edges; reduced motion shows
everything immediately without animation.

The intro sentence above Get started is also a button. Hover/focus highlights
Get started; clicking either starts the same challenge guide. The short bounty
and proposal links below it use the standard text-action color change.

Special effects belong to the symbol, introductory action and discovery areas.
Only hovering the logo symbol ignites the original ember. The wordmark only
changes color. Find new cryptography has a cursor-local glow with a small bounty
panel glow; Test its limits has localized text shimmer and a weekly-card glow.
Weekly cards keep their background color. Home and Schemes card hover/focus,
and tagline previews, use a soft glow without an added outline. There are no Home connecting arrows. Light mode uses pale
teal for the challenge card; dark mode keeps the forest-green treatment.

The points help popup is joined to the balance by a fine stem/notch, with a
short reveal animation. It has no shadow or hover underline. Only its arrow
moves. The weekly reward explanation appears on badge hover or keyboard focus.

Expanders use a chevron and open state. The shared Work with an agent entry
reveals the actual prompt, Copy prompt and a close cross. Successful copying
changes the native button to Copied briefly. Failure leaves selectable text
and an honest manual-copy instruction. No success banner is added.

An expanded guide consumes the first click outside its prompt and associated
controls: it hides without activating the background action. A subsequent click
can navigate. Guide highlights follow native rounded shapes. Keyboard focus
remains visible, and reduced-motion preferences remove decorative motion.
