REVIEWER SCORE ELIGIBILITY — 5 November 2026
Policy: crucible-reviewer-exclusion-v1

Configured reviewer accounts are not competitors. The application identifies
them by the existing settings.reviewer_ids account UUIDs, never by handle. An
access administrator who is not also a reviewer remains eligible.

Every reviewer score component and total is zero in the API, with rank=null and
score_status=reviewer. The interface shows Reviewer instead of points or rank.
The participant leaderboard excludes reviewers before counting or paginating,
and its ranking window includes only eligible participants. This applies to
recovery, scheme-admission, weekly, note-contribution and bounty points.

No migration or manual balance reset is needed. Scores are derived; original
submissions, notes, ratings, admission credits and bounty award receipts remain
intact. An old award's recorded amount is historical evidence, not an exception
to current eligibility. Note votes still express support for the note. Accepted
proposal counts and factual personal/first-recovery records remain visible.

Reviewer recoveries still establish the factual recovery frontier. Removing
them before detecting advances would incorrectly reward later copies. They
earn no points themselves and their milestone receipts never count toward a
bonus. Scheme recovery records and their original attribution are not rewritten.

Eligibility follows the current configured role. Removing reviewer status later
would restore scores derived from the retained evidence under the applicable
policy. Permanent exclusion after leaving the role, or excluding only work done
during a recorded reviewer tenure, would require a separate policy and durable
role history; neither is silently implied here.

Reviewer permissions, invitation membership and monthly bounty allowance are
unchanged. This is score eligibility, not a restriction on research or reading.
