What each argument establishes

Finite-field DH, teaching-dh-kem@7
  Correctness: exponent equality, exact prime/subgroup certificates, valid
  nonidentity encodings and identical framed hash input; zero disagreement in
  the stated mathematical honest experiment.
  Security: interval-CDH on the exact fixed group and exponent distribution.
  A classical hash-query extractor loses Q in success, giving whole-key
  recovery <= 2^-256 + Q*epsilon + delta, capped at one. Delta is an explicit
  concrete-hash transfer premise, not a result about SHA-256.

RSA, rsa-kem@4
  Correctness: CRT inversion for every residue, including zero and nonunits,
  after successful certified bounded key generation.
  Security: unit-RSA inversion for the actual emitted modulus distribution.
  Public verification of queried preimages avoids Q success loss, but its work
  is charged. Sampling all residues adds rho=E[1/p+1/q-1/(pq)]; the proof
  preserves the modulus distribution when weighting the unit event.

Elliptic-curve DH, dhkem-x25519@curve-compressed-v2
  Correctness: exact curve/group certificates, prime-order group identity and
  injective compressed-point encoding. These are custom curves, not X25519.
  Security: CDH on each actual registered curve, with the same Q-query loss and
  explicit SHA-256 transfer premise as finite-field DH.

Module lattice, ml-kem@compressed-research-v4
  Correctness: deterministic support proofs at degrees 8/16. The statistical
  bounds at 32/64 use concrete-to-independent-noise tail domination; 128/256
  additionally require the stated independent-rounding surrogate to bound the
  relevant residual event. Deterministic tensors are not literally independent.
  Security: two exact seeded noisy-equation hybrid premises, retaining the
  public seed, actual expander and joint noise correlations. Whole-key recovery
  <= epsilon_key + epsilon_ephemeral + Q_G/2^n + 2^-256 in the transform ROM;
  concrete inference needs an additional hash-transfer term.

Frodo, frodokem@anchor-research-v3
  Correctness: support proof at n=8; all larger registered n have an exact
  rational 2^-133 ceiling in the declared independent finite-CDF experiment.
  Transfer to seeded execution remains a visible one-sided premise.
  Security: two seeded noisy-matrix hybrids and distinct transform-hash roles.
  The final 128-bit key introduces Q_F/2^128 in addition to the native-message
  query and final-guess terms. Public salt is not counted as secret entropy.

Streamlined NTRU Prime, sntrup@3
  Correctness: coefficient bound 8w<q/2, exact centered lifting, mod-3 recovery
  and deterministic confirmation; all 27 configurations, including two outside
  the default progression, are covered by the stated mathematical argument.
  Security: exact rounded-core one-wayness with actual input prehash and
  confirmation retained. Query hiding gives the conservative term
  OW_core(T_B)+(2Q+1)/2^256, where Q counts all relevant domains and phases.

HQC, hqc@modeled-research-v3
  Correctness: stages 1–4 have a deterministic residual-support proof. Stages
  5–66 retain finite-expansion and decoder-event domination premises for the
  exact rational modeled bound. Average honest failure is not a worst-message
  CCA correctness guarantee.
  Security: exact native-PKE message recovery. Guessing the first target
  transform query loses q_G, but needs neither hidden encryption coins nor a
  candidate-verification oracle. The proof stops before answering that query.

Classic McEliece, classic-mceliece@length-research-v1
  Correctness: the binary Goppa distance argument, uniqueness through shortening,
  and the specified bounded-distance decoder cover all fifty lengths.
  Security: exact seeded-Goppa syndrome one-wayness, including structural
  attacks. Public weight/syndrome checks extract the target without a query
  success loss; candidate-check work is charged. No random-code hardness claim
  is substituted for the actual distribution.

Shared limits

Security statements concern one fresh honest ciphertext, classical queries and
recovery of the entire encapsulator key. DH/RSA/EC also give explicitly defined
one-challenge key indistinguishability. No decapsulation/answer-checking oracle,
leakage, quantum-query, multi-target or full IND-CCA theorem is claimed.
Mathematical correctness concerns valid successful generation and faithful
execution of specified algorithms. Native-code refinement, faults and resource
aborts are not established by an algebraic proof or finite test.

The hardness/transfer terms have no instantiated small values. Small playable
stages may give vacuous bounds. These reductions supply neither numerical
security levels nor lower bounds from the plotted attack-cost upper bounds.
Fixed hashes are not proved to be random oracles. Conventional independent LWE
is motivation, not an automatically applicable reduction for seeded variants.
