ML-KEM candidate ladder: correctness evidence and its limits
==========================================================

Status: a design and calculation artifact, not automatic profile admission.
No existing scheme version or challenge is changed by these files.

The intended challenge endpoint is the published ML-KEM-512 parameter set.
No separate768 deployment reference is selected. The512 standard endpoint has
its published category-1 assessment; lower-degree research configurations inherit
neither that category nor a full KEM reduction or implementation certificate.

Proposed simplest path
---------------------
Keep q=3329, module rank k=2, eta1=3, eta2=2, du=10 and dv=4 fixed. Change only
the power-of-two polynomial degree n through 8,16,32,64,128,256. Use every native
n-bit message, rather than restricting the message space of a larger ring.
The n=256 endpoint must conform byte-for-byte to the published ML-KEM-512
algorithms. All lower degrees are explicitly nonstandard research variants.
Six genuinely different degrees are more defensible than invented intermediate
degrees or several redundant parameter combinations merely to inflate tier count.
The sampling/NTT adaptation and its structural-security consequences require
separate review; this document addresses honest agreement only.

The wider candidate JSON also covers rank1 configurations and the alternative
ML-KEM-768 family (eta1=2, rank1..3), so rejected designs and their evidence remain
inspectable. Inclusion in that JSON does not recommend or activate a configuration.

Two distinct evidentiary statements
---------------------------------
1. Conservative-compression: an analytical upper bound on a single honest
   encryption/decryption experiment in the ideal independent sampling model.
   Ciphertext rounding is the actual deterministic rounding operation; its
   errors need not be independent, uniform, or independent of encryption noise.
   Small parameters also admit a deterministic support bound, requiring no
   probabilistic assumption about the supported noise draws.

2. Independent-uniform-u-compression: an exact analytical upper bound for a
   modified noise experiment. Each coefficient of the u-rounding error is drawn
   independently as Decompress(Compress(U))-U with U uniform modulo3329, and is
   independent of every noise coefficient. This is an additional modeling
   approximation. The bridge from this modified experiment to the actual KEM
   has NOT been established here, and exact rational arithmetic does not prove
   that bridge. The v-rounding error is bounded adversarially in this analysis;
   it is not replaced by an independent random variable.

Both probabilistic statements concern one honestly generated key and honest
encapsulation, averaged over their randomness. They are not a worst-key bound,
an adversarially selected-coin guarantee, an IND-CCA bound, or a statement about
many adaptive attempts. Finite seeded SHAKE/hash expansion is not literally
independent infinite randomness. Its idealized interpretation and any failure
of implementation fidelity remain explicit premises, as with other research
family analyses on Crucible. Operational generation aborts are separate.

The distinction is relevant even for the published scheme. Barbosa et al.,
"Formally Verified Correctness Bounds for Lattice-Based Cryptography", CCS2025,
verify computations for the heuristic ML-KEM noise experiment. Their Section1
and footnote4 distinguish that result from proving that the same numerical
bound applies to ML-KEM itself; they provide a looser bound under MLWE instead.
This is evidence about the scope of the standard-style numerical analysis,
not evidence of an exploitable failure in ML-KEM.
https://www.cryptojedi.org/papers/frodoec-20250831.pdf

The September2026 preprint by Duriez and Tommasini, "Dependency-Aware ROM/CBD
Correctness Bounds for ML-KEM-768 at the Heuristic Failure Scale", reports a
dependency-preserving2^-164.81 bound for honest fixed-message/random-message
ML-KEM-768 in an explicit ideal randomness model. It does not claim adaptive
correctness or a fixed-SHAKE equivalence. We have read its scope and statement,
but have not independently validated its proof or rerun its artifacts. It is
a relevant direction for improving this analysis, not a certificate for our
ML-KEM-512 endpoint or any reduced degree. Consequently the2025 paper's open
problem discussion should not be presented as a blanket claim about the state
of research today.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09983

FIPS203 Table1 supplies the published estimate approximately2^-138.8 for
ML-KEM-512. It is attributed to that parameter set only, never copied onto a
smaller-degree construction. Our independent conservative computation below
is intentionally looser even in its modified noise model.
https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.203.pdf

Algebraic argument
------------------
Work in R=Z_q[x]/(x^n+1), with independently sampled CBD_eta coefficients in
the ideal experiment. Let eta=eta1 and let CBD2 be used for e1 and e2.
With t=A*s+e, u=A^T*r+e1, v=t^T*r+e2+Encode(m), define centered compression
errors delta_u=Decompress(Compress(u))-u and delta_v analogously. The decoded
value before its one-bit rounding is Encode(m)+E, where

  E = e^T*r - s^T*(e1+delta_u) + e2 + delta_v  (mod q).

The script enumerates all3329 coefficients under the integer FIPS rounding
rules and verifies |delta_u|<=2 and |delta_v|<=104. For either encoded message
bit, a one-bit decoding error requires |E|>=832 for the corresponding integer
lift. (The exact positive and negative endpoints differ by one because q is
odd; taking832 is conservative.) Consequently a failure requires

  |W| >= 728,  W=e^T*r-s^T*(e1+delta_u)+e2.

A deterministic sufficient condition is

  nk * [eta^2 + eta*(eta2+2)] + eta2 +104 <832.

This proves zero disagreement for n8 and16 when k2,eta3. It is an incidental
property of these small dimensions, not a policy requiring zero failure.

Conservative-compression derivation
----------------------------------
Fix a coefficient of W and condition on A,r,e1. Then delta_u is fixed and is
independent of the remaining independently sampled s,e,e2. Each negacyclic
coefficient pairs each relevant e or s coefficient exactly once. For x>1,
write M_X(x)=E[x^X]. Symmetry of a CBD variable S implies M_S(x^a) increases
with |a|. Thus

  M_S(x^(e1_j+delta_u_j)) <= M_S(x^(|e1_j|+2)).

Integrating over the independent r and e1 coefficients gives

  E[x^W] <= ( M_(ER)(x) * M_(S*(|E1|+2))(x) )^(nk) * M_E2(x),

where E,R,S are independent CBD_eta and E1,E2 are independent CBD2.
This argument does not require delta_u to have any particular distribution.
All the dominating product distributions are symmetric, so the same expression
bounds the negative tail. A union bound over n message coefficients yields

  delta <= 2*n*x^-728 * (M_(ER)(x)*M_(S*(|E1|+2))(x))^(nk)*M_E2(x).

There is no assumption of independence between the n decoded coefficients.

Independent-uniform-u-compression derivation
------------------------------------------
Let D be the centered rounding error of one independent uniform coefficient.
Its exact integer counts over3329 inputs are

  D=-2:128, D=-1:1024, D=0:1024, D=1:1024, D=2:129.

Replace M_(S*(|E1|+2)) above by M_(S*(E1+D)). This product is symmetric because
S is symmetric, despite the one-count asymmetry in D. All products for a fixed
decoded coefficient are independent under the stated modified experiment, so
the Chernoff/union expression bounds that experiment. It does not, by itself,
bound the real u-rounding correlations.

Certificate mechanics and results
--------------------------------
All CBD masses are exact rational binomial coefficients; compression counts
are exhaustively enumerated. A floating-point search proposes only the witness
x>1, which is rounded to an explicit rational. The final probability expression
uses Python Fraction arithmetic, and the comparison to2^-128 uses integer
cross multiplication. A witness need not be optimal for its bound to be valid.
The displayed exponent is rounded DOWN using exact integer comparisons, giving
a conservative dyadic upper bound. The full rational's SHA256 is recorded so
it can be reproduced without storing thousands of decimal digits.

For k2,eta1=3,eta2=2,q3329,du10,dv4:

  n     conservative-compression      independent-uniform-u-compression
  8     0                            0
  16    0                            0
  32    <=2^-367                     <=2^-565
  64    <=2^-193                     <=2^-383
  128   <=2^-95                      <=2^-236
  256   <=2^-43                      <=2^-128

The model's endpoint Chernoff exponent is approximately128.041429812; the exact
rational comparison passes2^-128. This is NOT the published2^-138.8 estimate:
using a worst-case v error and Chernoff rather than the exact convolved tail
deliberately sacrifices tightness. A weak conservative bound, such as2^-43,
does not say the actual failure probability is near2^-43. It says this proof
technique does not establish the requested target at that configuration.
