  The adapters make no confidentiality claim. The later selected classical

  attack estimate or measured cost.
- Compare known attacks under a common goal, success target, resource profile,
  parameter distribution, and preprocessing policy. A reference benchmark should
  retain failures/timeouts and fresh independent instances, with pinned code.
  Measured attack cost, attributed estimates, and answer verification are distinct.
- DH uses a fixed group per tier while RSA generates fresh moduli. Separate
  preparation from online work and state any amortization across targets. An
  hourly deadline is not a cap on compute or prior preparation.

  Shared-secret recovery covers a narrower goal than general IND-CCA KEM security.
  Classical empirical results and quantum resource estimates need separate labels.
