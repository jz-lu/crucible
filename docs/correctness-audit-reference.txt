CURRENT CORRECTNESS EVIDENCE AUDIT — 17 OCTOBER 2026

Scope: the eight current research releases selected by crucible/registry.py.
This is a source/evidence inventory and mathematical-scope review, not a fresh
independent proof audit, formal verification, or attestation that every listed
test was rerun today. Historical reports remain evidence of their dated runs.

Conclusion

All eight releases state support for honest agreement. Six claim perfect
agreement under their stated mathematical/implementation premises. Frodo claims
a conditional statistical bound, with one perfect small profile. Module-v4
combines deterministic support arguments, conditional analytical bounds and an
explicit heuristic compression model across its six configurations. These claims
meet the required claimed <=2^-128 goal under their stated premises; this inventory
does not validate those premises or certify an executable.
None has an end-to-end machine-checked proof that the exact executable, compiler,
runtime, RNG and hardware implement the claim. All retain independent admission
or implementation-review gaps. "Every scheme is rigorously verified" would
therefore overstate the evidence. Round trips and benchmark samples cannot close
that gap or establish a negligible probability such as 2^-133 experimentally.

The common experiment is fresh KeyGen; honest Encaps; honest Decaps. Define
disagreement or honest rejection as failure, and separately define permitted
generation/resource aborts. A claim delta conditional on successful generation
does not prove unconditional availability. Abort probabilities are not universally
bounded by the existing proofs. Never resample disagreement into apparent success.

1. Finite-field DH / teaching-dh-kem@7 — mathematical identity, conditional zero

For the registered prime-order groups and nonzero honest x,r in the declared
interval, (g^x)^r=(g^r)^x. Both sides encode/hash the same transcript. Valid prime
and subgroup certificates also ensure honest inputs pass validation. Hash
collision resistance is unnecessary for equal-input agreement.

Evidence: crucible/dh_v7.py (validate_profile, keygen/encapsulate/decapsulate and
correctness manifest); tests/test_classical_frontier.py and the independent
transcript/model checks in test_classical_frontier_audit.py. Public prime
certificates are executable checks, not formal verification of this interpreter.
Gap: independent current-source implementation/admission review remains pending.

2. RSA / rsa-kem@4 — CRT/Fermat argument, conditional zero

Distinct certified primes and ed=1 mod (p-1)(q-1) give (z^e)^d=z mod N for every
z, including nonunits (prove separately modulo p and q, then use CRT). Matching
encodings/KDF give equal keys. The structured prime sampler is not uniform over
all primes, but that distinction does not invalidate this agreement identity.

Evidence: crucible/rsa_v4.py, tests/test_classical_frontier.py and
test_classical_frontier_audit.py; inherited certification/sampling tests in
test_rsa_v3.py are supporting evidence, not a replacement source audit.
Gap: the five-second bounded search may abort; no zero-abort claim follows.
Current executable correctness and independent admission are not certified.

3. Elliptic-curve DH / dhkem-x25519@curve-compressed-v2 — group identity,
   certified public parameters, conditional zero

The registered custom prime-order curves are not X25519. Public ECPP/Lucas
certificates plus a nonidentity point with qG=O and the Hasse interval establish
the intended order. For honest nonzero scalars, r(xG)=x(rG); compressed encoding,
decoding and transcript hashing must faithfully preserve that equality.

Evidence: crucible/ec_compressed.py, scripts/check_ec_parameters.py,
docs/implementation-ec-research.txt, tests/test_classical_frontier.py and
test_classical_frontier_audit.py. Some documents concern the predecessor's
uncompressed representation; the current compressed release needs its own tests.
Gap: arithmetic/encoding implementation is tested, not formally verified;
independent implementation/admission review remains pending.

4. Module lattice / ml-kem@compressed-research-v4 — mixed evidence,
   common <=2^-128 ceiling under explicit premises

The six settings vary ring degree8,16,32,64,128,256 while fixing rank2,q3329,
CBD3/CBD2 and ciphertext compression10/4. Completed endpoint operations match
FIPS203 ML-KEM-512. Reduced rings and their parameter-separated hash domains
are research constructions, not standardized ML-KEM or inherited security claims.

Degrees8/16 have deterministic support bounds. Degrees32/64 have conservative
conditional bounds <=2^-367 and <=2^-193 without a uniform-compression premise.
Degrees128/256 have modeled bounds <=2^-236 and <=2^-128 using independent
uniform-input u-compression errors; this is explicitly a heuristic bridge to the
concrete algorithm. All probabilistic calculations retain ideal independent
expanded CBD noise. v-compression uses a deterministic bound, not another
independence assumption. The endpoint also has published FIPS203 failure analysis.

Evidence: crucible/module_compressed_v4_core.py, crucible/lattice_module_v4.py,
docs/mlkem-correctness-candidates.txt and its exact certificate JSON, the rational
certificate checker, independent schoolbook arithmetic comparisons and NIST
ACVP vectors in tests/test_module_compressed_v4_core.py. The bounded matrix
sampler can abort; those events are availability failures, not honest disagreement.
Gap: exact model computation does not prove model adequacy. Finite conformance
vectors are not an exhaustive implementation proof; independent admission and
implementation review remain pending. Historical module-v3 source/manifests and
its separate deterministic-support argument remain intact.

5. Frodo / frodokem@anchor-research-v3 — conditional statistical certificate

The declared common bound is at most 2^-133 under independent finite-CDF noise
in an ideal-XOF model. For each of 64 coordinates, the analysis bounds
E'' + sum(S'*E-E'*S), then uses a union bound at the decoding threshold 4096.
An exact rational/integer Chernoff calculation avoids floating-point optimism.
The n=8 profile has the stronger deterministic bound
2*n*12^2+12=2316<4096 and is perfect on the stated support.

Evidence: docs/lattice-frodo-correctness.json, docs/frodo-anchor-ladder.txt,
crucible/lattice_frodo_v3.py, tests/test_lattice_parameter_frontier.py and
tests/test_frodo_anchor_ladder.py. The n=640 anchor has pinned native conformance
fixtures. Older frontier documents selecting n=592 are superseded by this
release's restored 80-profile n=8..640 domain.
Gap: the certificate is conditional on its independent-noise/ideal-XOF model;
it is not an unconditional theorem about every SHAKE-seeded executable run.
Finite honest samples cannot validate a 2^-133 tail, and native-anchor agreement
does not itself prove every smaller profile's executable behavior.

6. NTRU Prime / sntrup@3 — published algebraic argument with parameter
   certificates, conditional zero

The claimed NTRU Prime 2020 Theorem 4 bound |3fm+gr|<=8w<q/2 prevents centered
lifting loss. Mod-3 recovery and the confirmation/encoding layer then agree.
Pinned tuples require prime p,q, irreducibility, 2p>=3w and q>=16w+1 plus the
implementation-domain restrictions; key generation must finish successfully.

Evidence: crucible/ntru_frontier.py, docs/ntru-parameter-frontier.txt,
tests/test_ntru_frontier.py (independent Python decapsulation, published-anchor
vectors, sampler/parameter/artifact checks). Native source substitutions and
sampler faithfulness are part of the implementation obligation.
Gap: no independent end-to-end implementation or constant-time certification;
bounded generation availability is separate from the algebraic theorem.

7. HQC / hqc@growth-research-v2 — deterministic concatenated-code bound,
   conditional zero for this research family

The honest error weight is at most E=2w^2+w. Repeated RM(1,7) distance is 64r;
a wrongly decoded symbol requires at least 32r flipped bits, including ties.
E<32r(delta+1) therefore limits outer-symbol errors to delta, within the retained
RS(16+2delta,16) decoder's radius. Truncation cannot increase error weight.
All 27 frozen profiles satisfy the inequality. This is not a blanket claim about
published HQC profiles or later HQC revisions.

Evidence: crucible/code_hqc_v2.py, docs/hqc-growth.txt, certified parameter data,
tests/test_hqc_growth.py and the separate native decoder-boundary harness.
Gap: the proof relies on the actual nearest-codeword/RS decoder implementing the
declared decoding guarantee; boundary tests do not prove all possible inputs.
Independent admission and full implementation review remain pending.

8. Classic McEliece / classic-mceliece@length-research-v1 — Goppa-code
   distance/decoding argument, conditional zero

The specified irreducible binary Goppa construction has distance at least
2t+1=129 for t=64, so every honest weight-64 error has a unique decoding target.
Shortened support length varies while the field, extension and decoder are fixed.
Unique decodability alone is not an executable proof: the retained decoder and
KEM encodings/transform must return that target on the entire admitted domain.

Evidence: crucible/code_native.py, crucible/code_mceliece.py, docs/code-research.txt,
tests/test_code_research.py, sampler checks and the published-endpoint KAT.
Gap: native decoder/general-parameter substitutions and successful systematic
key generation remain premises. No independent end-to-end admission certificate.

Implications for proposals and benchmark publication

Require a precise failure experiment, full parameter coverage and a claimed
bound at most 2^-128 (or perfect agreement), with named premises and supporting
reasoning. Conditional, model-based and motivated conjectural justifications are
eligible; prior proof of every premise is not required. Report generation aborts
separately and bind implementation evidence to exact source/build identities. Reviewers
must distinguish: abstract theorem; parameter certificate; numerical bound;
conformance/negative tests; empirical samples; and manual review status.

A family proof may cover many configurations without redundant per-stage papers.
Statistical claims may rest on explicit unproved assumptions; distinguish a
rigorous calculation inside a model from conjectural links to the actual scheme.
Do not force artificial zero-failure constructions merely for a badge.
Metadata validation and reviewer checkboxes alone do not verify a proof.
Benchmark agreement checks add finite implementation observations only. Their
results must not promote a correctness or confidentiality claim automatically.

A demonstrated actual failure probability above 2^-128 refutes the target for its
stated scope. A loose upper bound, missing argument or counterexample to a premise
undermines the justification without necessarily proving the actual target false.
One observed failing instance is not by itself a probability bound; statistical
correctness allows rare failures. See correctness-policy-v1.txt for these review
distinctions. This audit adds no automated refutation or attack pipeline.
