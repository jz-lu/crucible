QUALITY LIMITS AND NEXT DECISION

The baseline contains many short, self-explanatory examples. The ten-case set was
written and frozen before its author saw baseline outputs; it adds unadorned
solver code, confident mathematical error, French metadata, ordinary academic
acknowledgment and a long paired injection context. It then informed the v2
clarification, so its v2 result is development/regression evidence, not untouched
validation. There was one sample per case per prompt, not a stability estimate.

These runs support continuing with Luna as the inexpensive pilot candidate.
They do not establish deployment accuracy, comprehensive abuse coverage or a
scientific-quality judgment. Before real automatic publication, exercise fresh
representative cases, the remaining authority/specimen boundary and faithful
explanations; then use a separately consented local/shadow trial and the reviewed
release path. Keep human fallback and operator disable behavior intact.

even while browsing and saving remain responsive. Measure queue age separately.

IMPLEMENTATION FOLLOW-UP AND FRESH BOUNDARY CHECK (2026-11-04–05)

The unchanged v2 prompt was tested on four newly written boundary cases after the
initial evaluation: all three ordinary discussion/incident-report/prior-review
cases passed and the operative concealment/administrator override was flagged.
The cases were frozen before these calls; labels were not sent to the model.
This narrow result supports the distinction but does not erase the earlier
ambiguous-case disagreement or establish general deployment accuracy.

A real-provider end-to-end rehearsal on a disposable SQLite application database
confirmed exact submitted-revision publication, continued privacy of earlier/later
drafts, a flagged note remaining private in the human queue, a subsequent human
decision, and a fresh worker invocation making no duplicate completed call. It
used two real calls and a $0.10 durable cap; known usage cost was $0.000521. It is
not an image-level deployment, browser-provider exercise or process-crash test.

Three operator-harness corrections preceded that final run: refreshing synthetic
sessions after switching clocks, supplying the note API's required nonblank
conditions, and removing a duplicated helper argument for a human decision. The
first two made no provider calls; the third reached both correct model outcomes
but did not retain their usage receipt. Those two calls are conservatively charged
their full $0.02710 reservation in the follow-up evidence. They are not production
defects or silently discarded model results.

Implementation review also fixed lease checks after real lock waits, repeated
authorization checks after input preparation, and permanent provider-error
classification. A callback savepoint prevents expired publication writes while
retaining known model usage/result evidence. Six real PostgreSQL 17 checks passed,
including a row-lock delay crossing lease expiry. No schema change was needed.

The reviewer now has a compact, collapsed screening panel on the exact revision.
