Lossless module-LWE research KEM — release modulus-research-v1
Assessment date2026-10-14. Local research beta, not scientific admission.

Why this is a separate construction
----------------------------------
The family page retains ML-KEM as its published reference; its immutable
standardized adapters remain available separately. The research release has
its OWN identity, encodings, public matrix sampler and parameters. It must not
be called standardized ML-KEM, a byte-compatible ML-KEM variant, or a scheme
covered automatically by the original security theorem.

All profiles retain degree256, full CBD eta2 noise, full256-bit key-generation
seed, full256-bit private rejection seed, full256-bit message and shared key,
and a complete reencryption/implicit-rejection transform. Tier1 uses module
rank1; tiers2-50 use rank3. Rank1 is the natural ring-LWE special case and reduces
the first-tier attack lattice from1536 to512 coordinates; this is not a
validated solve-time guarantee. The remaining
profiles keep rank fixed and change the prime modulus q. No seed, message,
noise seed or output key is entropy-truncated. Tiers are frozen profiles, not
claims of equal measured work. Recovery does not backfill other profiles.

The q ladder decreases from the Mersenne prime2^127-1 to47339. Increasing q
makes fixed small errors relatively easier to distinguish/recover; the cost of
encoding q is consequently HIGHER at easier tiers. This is intentional and
explains why security and public-key size need not increase together. The
rank1-to-rank3 transition also affects sizes. The graphs should report actual
encodings, rather than synthesize a monotone cost curve.

Source primitives and exact specification
-----------------------------------------
FIPS203 algorithms and ML-KEM-768 motivate rank3, degree256, CBD2,
SHA3-256/SHA3-512/SHAKE256, message encoding and the reencryption transform.
Primary source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.203.pdf
This derivative explicitly differs as follows:
* q is a profile-specific prime, rather than3329.
* A is sampled UNIFORMLY IN THE COEFFICIENT DOMAIN, not ML-KEM's NTT domain.
* Public keys include a header and q; coefficients are losslessly serialized.
* Ciphertexts contain full u and v coefficients; no lossy compression.
* Tier1 has rank1. Other profiles have rank3.

Ring: R_q=Z_q[x]/(x^256+1). k is the registered module rank, eta=2.
Hash definitions: H=SHA3-256, G=SHA3-512, J=SHAKE256(output32).
Keygen randomness: d32||z32. rho||sigma=G(d||uint8(k)).
A[i,j] comes from SHAKE128("Crucible-Module-LWE-v1/A" || uint128le(q) ||
rho32 || uint8(i) || uint8(j)). Consume fixed-width little-endian candidates,
mask to bit_length(q), and REJECT those>=q. Never reduce a nonuniform candidate
modulo q. The finite expansion cap aborts without substituting another sampler.
CBD2(PRF(sigma,nonce)) sums two bits minus two independent bits for each
coefficient; PRF=SHAKE256(seed32||nonce1,128). Nonces0..k-1 give s and k..2k-1
give e. t=A*s+e. Public key is "MLW1"||uint128le(q)||rho32||Encode(t).
Each polynomial encodes256 coefficients in increasing order; each coefficient
uses ceil(bit_length(q)/8) bytes, little-endian, and must be<q.
The private serialization is z32||signed-int8(s)||pk||H(pk).

Encap draws the complete message mu32. K||coins=G(mu||H(pk)). Sample r,e1,e2
from coins using nonces0..k-1,k..2k-1,2k. Compute u=A^T*r+e1 and
v=t^T*r+e2+EncodeMessage(mu). Message bit i is coefficient ceil(q/2)*bit_i,
with LSB-first bit order within each byte. Ciphertext=Encode(u)||Encode(v).
Return the complete K32, with no truncation or replacement.

Decap subtracts s^T*u from v, rounds each coordinate to the nearest message
center modulo q, computes G(mu'||H(pk)), and reencrypts the entire ciphertext.
Return K' on equality; otherwise J(z||ct). A length-correct noncanonical
ciphertext also returns that implicit-rejection key. Wrong lengths/types are
format errors. Private/public inputs are fixed to the requested profile.
There is no public decapsulation oracle in Crucible.

Perfect correctness for every actual profile
--------------------------------------------
The error polynomial is e^T*r-s^T*e1+e2. Each product contributes at most
n*eta^2 to an individual coefficient, even with negacyclic signs. Thus
  |error_i| <= 2*k*n*eta^2+eta <=6146.
Every registered prime satisfies floor(q/4)>6146; the smallest is47339, with
margin11834>6146. There is no lossy compression term. Consequently every honest
message bit decodes correctly for every allowed noise draw. This proof does
not rely on sample independence or a simulated failure rate. Exact encoding,
faithful arithmetic and full reencryption then give equal shared keys.
Implementation bugs and resource aborts remain distinct from this mathematical
statement. Every issued challenge checks honest agreement once and retains the
original encapsulated target, even if that check were to fail.

Calibration and its material limits
-----------------------------------
Frozen primes and actual estimator outputs are docs/lattice-module-calibration.json.
The target8+120(t-1)/49 chooses q; it is not reported as the actual cost.
Offline calibration uses the pinned official lattice-estimator commit
53da5982597709ba0fdf94ea37a84d822310fd84, archiveSHA256
4236adc38031c139c8a2721804d8962d6cacd015fae37f6f5215d2ac7f011060, Sage10.1.
https://github.com/malb/lattice-estimator/tree/53da5982597709ba0fdf94ea37a84d822310fd84
LWE.estimate.rough takes flattened n768,m768,CBD2 secret/CBD2 error, classical
ADPS16 Core-SVP and GSA, returning primal uSVP and dual hybrid estimates.
The endpoint minimum is approximately127.978777 reference bits, not exactly128.
The first two low-cost profiles omit numerical estimates: the heuristic's
minimum block-size floor is not credible evidence of their tiny attack cost.
Rank1 Tier1 has no flattened rank3 estimate at all.

Coarse exponent interpolation selects candidate primes only. Every reported
number is recomputed by the estimator at that exact prime. All50 primes were
proved with Sage is_prime(proof=True)/next_prime(proof=True). The source archive
and every extracted file are checked before estimator execution.
Reproduce using Sage:
  sage -python scripts/lattice_calibrate_module.py \
    --estimator /path/to/lattice-estimator-53da5982597709ba0fdf94ea37a84d822310fd84 \
    --archive /path/to/pinned-estimator.tar.gz --output /tmp/module-calibration.json

This flattened LWE approximation does not account for every use of the module
structure, partial information in v, multi-target advantage, other attacks,
full KEM reduction, quantum attacks or side channels. Core-SVP is not a
hardware-operation counter and does not predict seconds. A perfect correctness
proof provides NO confidentiality guarantee. In particular this construction
is not admitted for cryptographic deployment.

Implementation evidence and remaining work
------------------------------------------
The self-contained Python implementation uses standard-library hash primitives
and OS randomness, without native crypto dependencies. It runs in a bounded
20-second process; stdout is restricted to the exact allowed payload length.
Its registered source hash is rechecked before execution. The artifact records
Python/runtime identity, not a complete closure over OS/system libraries.
It is variable-time and not a hardened cryptographic library.

Tests cover all50 actual profiles, complete CBD2 four-bit distribution,
rejection-vs-modulo sampling, canonical encodings, negacyclic wrap signs,
full implicit rejection including malformed coefficients, source changes and
resource deadlines. Independent vectors come from scripts/lattice_module_reference.py,
which uses Sage's finite-field polynomial quotient arithmetic and independently
implements sampling/serialization. Six profiles span both ranks and multiple
coefficient widths. These are generated research vectors, NOT NIST KATs.
The immutable published ML-KEM adapter retains its separate official ACVP/KAT
evidence. This new derivative cannot reuse that evidence as a full-conformance
claim. Independent cryptographic review and analysis of this precise parameter
and transform family remain required before scientific admission.

Public-input solver demonstration status
---------------------------------------
scripts/lattice_solve_module_tier1.py is an EXPERIMENTAL lattice/CVP sketch,
not a verified example solution. It expands only the public ring equations,
runs fpylll LLL/nearest-plane, reconstructs a candidate message, and requires
complete ciphertext reencryption before returning a key. It has no access to
private generation coins or the server target. The first rank3 experiment was
stopped without completion; the final rank1 experiment was also stopped within
a short manual test budget without a result. A fast-double LLL attempt reported
a numerical reduction error. No public-input successful recovery or Tier1
solve-time claim is established for this construction. This limitation is
separate from the completed full-KEM correctness/KAT and all-profile tests.
Do not interpret the absence of a completed example attack as security evidence.
