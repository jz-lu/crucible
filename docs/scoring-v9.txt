CRUCIBLE — PROVISIONAL POINTS POLICY v9
Decision date: 17 October 2026
Policy ID: crucible-points-v9

This revision explicitly replaces the HQC recovery enrollment with
hqc@modeled-research-v3, configurations 1..66. It leaves the v8 incentive formula,
the other seven enrolled releases and their frozen weights unchanged. See
docs/scoring-v8.txt for that earlier decision and full calculation rules.

The HQC replacement uses a different parameter construction and a conditional
correctness model. Its configuration numbers do not translate the old release's
recoveries. A new enrollment is therefore a deliberate policy change, not an
automatic consequence of adding profiles to the registry.

CURRENT HQC ENROLLMENT

Exact release:      hqc / modeled-research-v3
Exact profile IDs:  hqc-modeled-research-v3-tier-1 through
                    hqc-modeled-research-v3-tier-66
Personal weight:    W(i) = floor(500 * i / 66), frozen as 66 literal integers
Record inheritance: disabled; first place belongs to the exact recovered profile

For example W(1)=7, W(5)=37, W(24)=181 and W(66)=500. These are incentive
weights, not estimates of equal security, attack cost or research value. Every
configuration is positive. Runtime scoring uses the pinned exact-profile table,
not the displayed navigation stages or a dynamically calculated denominator.
Adding, hiding or relabelling stages cannot change an enrolled weight. A further
release or configuration requires another explicit enrollment decision.

Each enrolled family still contributes at most 2,500 recovery points:
  Personal progress = the greatest recovered weight, at most 500.
  First-record bonus = four times the greatest weight where first, at most 2,000.

Only one maximum of each kind is counted per family. Repeated hourly recoveries
and a sequence of intermediate solves do not add their weights together.
Recovering an exact-only higher HQC profile does not imply a lower-profile solve
or take its first place. The separate admitted-new-family award remains 1,000;
replacing HQC's release does not award a new-family contribution.

HISTORICAL EVIDENCE AND POLICY

HQC growth-research-v2 is no longer enrolled for current recovery scores. Its
receipts, methods, timestamps, historical standings and original manifests remain
unchanged and accessible. They earn no v9 points, do not reserve places in the new
release and are not converted into new configurations. A participant may retain
an archived personal record in their profile until they have a current-release
record, but the archived result remains unscored. There is no automatic transfer
or simultaneous recovery award for both HQC releases.

The v8 HQC enrollment, including its original 27 weights, remains in
HISTORICAL_FAMILY_RELEASES in crucible/scoring.py solely as audit data. It is not
joined into current score calculations. docs/scoring-v8.txt is retained as the
historical policy document. Current totals are derived again from stored evidence
under v9; no database migration or receipt rewrite is needed. API responses state
the current policy ID.

The seven unchanged enrollments are finite-field DH@7, RSA@4,
elliptic-curve DH@curve-compressed-v2, module lattice@compressed-research-v4,
FrodoKEM@anchor-research-v3, Streamlined NTRU Prime@3 and
Classic McEliece@length-research-v1. Their v8 weights and inheritance rules remain
exactly the same. All eight current families have equal 500/2,000 ceilings;
this deliberately says nothing about equal difficulty or scientific merit.
