MinRank candidate — bounded research review, 2026-10-14

Recommendation: a serious research candidate, not an admission or implementation.
MinRank adds a rank-metric direction to the proposed lattice/Hamming-code roster.
Different problems do not imply independent security. Being recent or lacking
standardization is not a reason to exclude a research proposal from Crucible.

Concrete constructions

Chatterjee, Mu and Vasudevan, Public-Key Encryption from the MinRank Problem
(2025), construct PKE from planted generic MinRank, with the specified uniform
matrix/error distributions and search-to-decision machinery. This is a direct
reason not to describe MinRank as supporting only signatures. [M1]

Debris-Alazard, Gaborit, Neveu and Ruatta, A Minrank-based Encryption Scheme à la
Alekhnovich-Regev (2025), give practical MinRankPKE and author implementation.
Its reduction uses stationary-MinRank; concrete analysis also treats MinRank
Support Learning (MSL). These must not be relabeled plain generic MinRank. Its
published dimensions, ranks and field choices provide an engineering starting
point, not a pre-existing 50-tier ladder. This is my preferred first feasibility
investigation because there is executable author code. [M2, M3]

Both are PKE constructions. A Crucible KEM target needs an explicit, reviewed
PKE-to-KEM transform, input entropy, encoding, hash/domain separation and game.
Encrypting a random message can define a passive recovery experiment; doing so
does not automatically justify a CCA-security claim. No such wrapper was
implemented or reviewed in this task.

Correctness and tier feasibility

For practical MinRankPKE, the bounded-rank noise must fit the Gabidulin decoder's
guaranteed radius. The paper states a condition involving r*d and
floor((ell_2-kappa)/2). Its Proposition 3 says "smaller than", while its first
parameter table reaches equality. Reconcile that wording with the decoder
theorem and actual implementation before fixing a tier certificate. Preserve
the exact field and sampling distributions, code-length/dimension conditions,
and complete KEM transform. The paper's asymptotic or benchmark values must not
be stretched into an unsupported linear security scale. Tests are implementation
evidence; the mathematical bound must cover every admitted reduced profile.

Published performance measurements are hardware- and implementation-specific.
They are not Crucible's comparative benchmark, and full-sized parameters alone
do not establish a usable low-tier challenge family.

Related names and attacks

MIRA, MiRitH and Mirath are signatures, outside the current KEM target. ROLLO and
RQC are actual rank-metric encryption/KEM constructions, but their structured
code distributions and exact reductions require separate descriptions. Rank
decoding and MinRank have precise relationships; sharing the word "rank" is not
an equivalence of every assumption or security claim. [M4, M5, M6]

EGMC/MinRank-Gabidulin is another distinct construction. A 2026 primary attack
reports breaks of all 16 proposed EGMC parameter sets. That does not establish
a break of either new construction above. A targeted current search found no
primary-source break of those two constructions; this is not an exhaustive
cryptanalysis survey or a security certification. An already-broken target may
still be useful if clearly identified as a historical attack exercise. [M7]

Origin and admission

These are previously identified proposals, not Crucible discoveries. Importing
them, adding a wrapper, or writing a tier generator does not create a newly
discovered assumption or automatically earn a new-family contribution award.
Follow the same exact-revision correctness, implementation, resource, sampler,
cryptanalysis and manual-review gates as other proposals. See
admission-and-coverage-challenge.txt and ../correctness-policy-v0.txt.
