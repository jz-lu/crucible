What each endpoint actually means
---------------------------------
DH: registered certified 3072-bit field, subgroup and short-exponent interval,
with a custom transcript KDF. The rho/NFS/interval reference is incomplete and
does not establish full-KEM confidentiality. Perfect algebraic agreement.

RSA: 3072-bit modulus, fresh certified balanced factors from the declared
structured distribution, exponent 65537 and custom transcript KDF. Its restricted
factoring reference is not a standardized RSA-KEM security reduction. Perfect
algebraic agreement under the specified generation and arithmetic.

Elliptic curves: 50 custom certified short-Weierstrass curves with compressed
points and a custom transcript KDF. The endpoint is near a generic-rho reference;
these are not 50 resized versions of X25519. Exact RFC 9180 DHKEM remains a
separately identified reference. Perfect agreement under valid curve arithmetic.

Module lattice: degrees 8,16,32,64,128,256 at the fixed rank/noise/compression of
ML-KEM-512. The final configuration matches FIPS 203 ML-KEM-512; reduced degrees
inherit no NIST security category. The shared <=2^-128 correctness claim includes
explicit noise/compression assumptions where required. See module-v4-design.txt
and mlkem-correctness-candidates.txt; a loose upper bound is not a failure proof.

Frodo: dimensions 8 through 640 by 8, retaining the salted Frodo640 construction,
noise distribution and message space. The endpoint is the published reference;
reduced dimensions are experimental. Its <=2^-133 claim uses the stated ideal
independent-noise model. Initial unestimated settings remain unestimated.

NTRU: 27 reviewed tuples through sntrup653 (653,4621,288), not the originally
selected sntrup761 endpoint. Ring and correctness certificates support perfect
honest agreement. The restricted hybrid-inclusive estimate and full uniform
research sampler do not transfer all claims about an SSH deployment or other
published sampler. See ntru-parameter-frontier.txt.

HQC: 66 configurations through the exact August 2025 HQC-1 parameters and wire
format, with secret/encryption/error weights 66/75/75 at the endpoint. A restricted
45-codec search replaces the old equal-weight, zero-failure ladder. Every row has
an exact arithmetic certificate for a conditional <=2^-128 failure bound. Actual
ring noise is correlated; conservativity of the independent-noise surrogate is
a separately stated conjectural premise, not established by the certificate.
The October 2026 revision and newer attacks change the current security picture.
This historical endpoint is not advertised as a current calibrated128-bit bound.
See hqc-model-v3.txt, including source versions and native conformance evidence.

McEliece: lengths 840..3488 with m=12,t=64, through the published348864 anchor.
Goppa distance supports perfect honest decoding under valid generation. Joint
m/n/t optimization is unresolved. Recent structural key recovery makes current
security reassessment necessary; the page supplies no numerical security curve.
See mceliece-parameter-review-20261017.txt for primary sources and a bounded plan.
That plan is not a completed attack-cost study or a replacement parameter ladder.

Shared admission and evidence practice
-------------------------------------
Every admitted configuration must claim zero honest disagreement or probability
at most2^-128, with exact distribution, scope, parameter coverage and assumptions.
Modeled/conjectural premises are allowed when explicit and independently
motivated. Machine checks do not certify those premises. Unknown/weaker claims
stay draft-only; implementation review and safe trusted integration remain
separate gates. No participant proposal source executes as part of intake.

The current audit does not remove three open scientific/operational boundaries:
1. Complete attack models and equal-security parameter optimization remain open,
   most clearly for HQC and McEliece. Conditional correctness-model transfer also
   remains open for the relevant lattice/code configurations.
2. Source tests, vectors and local worker checks are not independent full
   implementation review, side-channel certification or formal verification.
3. The reviewed native runtime is still macOS arm64. A Linux deployment needs its
   own reviewed build artifacts and validation; portable CI is source-only.

Service and measurement changes in this pass
-------------------------------------------
- Exact JSON copy and protocol display preserve large integers.
- All families use one generation-attempt ledger. Declared resource exhaustion
  remains retryable; malformed results, changed provenance and unexplained
  worker faults hold the profile. Honest disagreement is recorded separately.
- Current policy v9 fixes a500-point progress ceiling and2000-point best-first
  ceiling per family. This is incentive weighting, not equal-security scoring.
  Old HQC receipts remain visible but do not transfer to its different release.
- Background catalogue refresh transfers dynamic records only; full metadata
  reloads when its revision changes, preserving archived links and access gates.
- Timing publications remain explicitly selected, bound to their exact release
  and implementation, and measured separately from process startup. Missing or
  aborted measurements are not interpolated into invented results.
