Crucible research landscape — catalogue and integration design
Reviewed 2026-10-17

This is the dated map-design record. Its original collections, scoring snapshot
and prospective mechanisms are historical context, not the current execution
contract. Agents start at GET /api/agent/pipeline; use /api/schemes and the
current scoring policy for live release identities and eligibility. The bundled
notes now also include release/display evidence; read notes.json for membership.

SCOPE

The map is available at /map/index.html through Research notebook → Scheme map.
It uses the existing beta admission boundary. Its curated, read-only research
notes are kept in this directory in the global notebook format; they are not
inserted into anyone's private notebook. Scoring and accounts are unchanged.

The map is a view over research notes. Literature notes describe existing work;
original research and procedural notes share the same global format. External
research does not need to be proposed to Crucible again. Curators verify sources,
identify the construction and edition, and give it a defensible location. That
is distinct from reviewing an original Crucible proposal or making a playable
challenge. A paper's existence is not a certification of its security.

WHAT THE RESEARCH DISTINCTION CHANGES

Discovery: find a mechanism for public-key advantage, with an exact assumption
and distribution. Exotic mathematics is neither necessary nor sufficient.
Development: improve an existing mechanism's construction, parameter choices,
correctness, cost, or implementation, explaining any change in assumptions.
Analysis: establish a proof, attack, benchmark, limitation or independent check.
This matters even when no construction changes.

These are overlapping research questions, not a ranking or mandatory sequence.
Costs, independent analysis, correctness and malicious-input behavior remain
separate questions; avoid collapsing them into a single maturity number.

FAMILIES AND RELATIONSHIPS

Broad areas are browsing regions, not independent security foundations. Plain
LWE and module LWE remain distinct families, with a comparison link. Different
rank-metric distributions must not be treated as interchangeable.

The visible layout gives each entry one primary home. Related-entry links cross
those boundaries. Each link states its meaning and cites its sources: comparison,
construction lineage or a shared project must not be mistaken for a reduction.
No graph distance or number of families is used as a robustness score.

A new construction merits a separate record when it adds a meaningful research
question or changes the mathematical bet. Parameter sets normally live beneath
the same record. Separate implementations retain their own identities. Splitting or
renaming records must not itself generate new scoring credit.

TWO SOURCES OF ENTRIES

1. Literature: external specification/paper, exact edition, authors and stable
   identifiers, source-backed summary, assumption overview and relations.
   No community proposal is required. Source metadata can be curated and corrected.
2. Crucible discoveries: an author's notebook work, with its provenance and review
   history. Do not label known literature a Crucible discovery. A reviewed record
   may link to multiple notes, implementations and external predecessors.

Both sources can enter the same map. Map inclusion, challenge implementation,
scoring admission and highlighting are distinct operations. All is inclusive but
curated, not a catalogue of every claimed hard problem or every paper.

RECORDS TO KEEP SEPARATE

Assumption record: exact distribution, attacker task, public information,
permitted queries, parameter regime, and relationships to known problems.
The current catalogue's assumption labels are explicitly only overviews.

Construction record: algorithms, secret/public information, correctness argument,
failure conditions, malformed-input behavior and the precise security game.
A theoretical PKE result is not automatically a fully specified KEM.

Release/configuration record: immutable implementation and specification versions,
canonical parameter identifiers, natural progression, serialization and verifier.
A challenge targets an exact release and game, not a mutable family label.

Evidence record: exact target, code/method, resources, environment, results,
limitations, and review status. Distinguishing, private-key recovery, shared-secret
recovery and an oracle-dependent attack must remain separate achievements.

Selection decision: which release is highlighted, why it adds value relative to
nearby entries, decision maker, supporting notes, effective dates, and appeal or
audit record. A review of the selection process is a valid procedural note.

SCORING AND CONTINUITY

Scoring admission can be broader than highlighting. It still needs a runnable
challenge, a correct verifier, stable parameter definitions, resource expectations
and review of the published attack surface. Nothing in this draft performs that
review or creates any new scored implementation.

The agreed direction is to retain legitimately earned credit and eligibility
history, even when attention moves. This is not yet implemented by this map.
Stop issuing an obsolete release if appropriate; preserve its results and rules.
Auditable corrections for invalid results/verifiers are separate from changing
research priorities. Do not promise to award new work indefinitely on a retired
or known-broken release.

A highlighted-challenge bonus should be prospective, with its rate and time
window recorded before work begins. Removing highlighting must not take away an
earned bonus. No bonus percentage is settled or activated here. Store immutable
awards and rule versions rather than recalculating history from current labels.

When a construction or progression changes: publish a new release, give advance
notice where possible, let started rounds finish under their recorded rules, and
explain which evidence transfers and why. Never silently carry a solved stage
across distributions merely because its number matches. Maintain the construction
identity and history while allowing its implementations to evolve.

NOTEBOOK AND PRIVACY

The durable notebook contains literature descriptions as well as original work
and deliberation. A literature note is not a new-scheme proposal. Private notes remain
private. Community visibility requires the configured reviewer's approval,
initially countableclouds; approval to share is separate from mathematical review.

A live integration should reference note_id, exact revision, content hash, author
and approval record. A later edit must not silently replace reviewed evidence.
Keep attachments immutable by hash with size/type limits and durable storage;
never execute uploaded research code in the web process. Backups and restore
checks are part of durability, independent of GitHub project hosting.

Map projections must respect notebook access control. Public or beta-visible
records must not leak private note titles, snippets, tags, counts, author links
or artifact URLs. External source metadata may be independently public, but it
does not make a private research note public. Approval decisions need immutable
attribution and timestamps. Withdrawn visibility must propagate to projections.

Every entry is now pinned to a local descriptive note revision. No local note
is presented as a saved server note or an approved publication/decision. The old
candidate-template.json is an unconnected design artifact, not an API request or
a required publication workflow, and is no longer offered in the map interface.

IMPLEMENTATION

notes.json holds 26 notes in the existing global format; catalogue.json holds
organization and bindings for 13 KEM entries. build.py validates their content
hashes and emits catalogue.js and notes-data.js. Notes are read at notes.html;
the map at index.html remains a separate projection. See NOTE-FORMAT.txt.
app.js uses text nodes, no remote requests, authentication or mutation endpoints.
The generated payload lets index.html work directly with file://. The CSP rejects
external scripts and connections. Source links are navigated only when clicked.

The notebook's Scheme map uses the network visualization. Table, branch and card
experiments remain implementation artifacts, not additional navigation choices.
Graph position and distance make no security or independence claims.

Each eligible entry carries a Scored badge, separate from the red highlighting
marker. The badge denotes points eligibility in the dated policy snapshot.

Details stay closed until selected, with a compact dialog linking to the full
note and, when available, the challenge. Source prose remains in notes.

RESEARCH REVIEW

Continue this research on a note offers a full agent task download. Proposals &
reviews contains exact-revision requests. The reviewer assesses a question using frozen
sources; source checks, mathematical checks and reproduction are separate fields.
Private requests are visible to the requester and configured reviewers only.
Requests can recommend map, scoring, highlighting or procedural decisions but
cannot activate those changes. Formal scheme admission remains a separate gate.
See NOTE-FORMAT.txt and docs/research-notebook-draft.txt for limits and routes.

VALIDATION LIMITS

Data integrity, revision hashes, notebook permissions and review workflows have
automated checks. Interface checks exercise mocked requests rather than making
research decisions on behalf of real participants. Source checks are scoped in
each note; they are not a comprehensive cryptanalytic audit or proof review.
